% letterspaced capitals like the title page's secondary block.
%
% Deliberate choices, all settled by rendering at final size (2026-09-08):
%   - TWO orbits, both tilted, never three and never one horizontal. Three

%     logo and the AEC/IAEA seal; the atom motif itself is generic, but that
%     particular configuration is spoken for.
%   - Nucleus at 0.65mm radius. It reads as a centre down to about 0.75 and as
%     a speck below 0.55; 0.65 is the light end of what still holds.
%   - Orbits at 0.22mm: thinner and they fall under the footer strip's own scan
%     lines, which are the same weight.
%
% Rebuild:
%   lualatex your-logo.tex
%   pdfseparate your-logo.pdf tmp-%d.pdf
%   mv tmp-1.pdf your-logo-kraft.pdf ; mv tmp-2.pdf your-logo-deepblue.pdf
